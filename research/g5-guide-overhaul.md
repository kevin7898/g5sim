# G5 Field Guide: what it shows, and the G5Link overhaul

Source: *G5 Display Field Guide* (independent training companion, Nov 2026, 67 pages), read page by page. The guide
reproduces Deere screenshots and on-screen help icons; we only describe them here and draw our own icons.

## What the guide's screens show

**Shortcut bar** (pp. 5, 43): SETUP, WORK, AUTOTRAC, GUIDANCE, QUICK LINE, SWAP TRACK, AUTO TURN / iTEC, ISOBUS VT,
HELP, MENU (green, last).
- Only keys that **toggle a function** have the status strip (WORK OFF, AUTOTRAC ON/OFF, iTEC OFF). Keys that **open
  an app** (SETUP, GUIDANCE, QUICK LINE, SWAP TRACK, ISOBUS VT, HELP) have no strip. The guide says to "confirm which
  shortcuts open an app versus toggle a function".
- AUTO keys: a round LED (grey off, green on) and the word AUTO across the top of the icon area (WORK, AUTO TURN).
- Icons are black line drawings on the light key; labels are small bold capitals.
- AUTOTRAC pie: white disc, black cross, quarters fill **blue** clockwise from the top right; strip green "ON" when
  engaged.

**Guidance app icon** (p. 26): white rounded tile, a yellow disc with a black arrow between guide lines, and the
yellow/green bottom stripe every app tile has. Steer **ON/OFF master toggle**: dark selected half, cream other half.

**Map** (pp. 5, 30, 43): round dark olive buttons with a light ring (swap layer, split map, legend list, flag,
notes, north arrow). Overhead and perspective view buttons are small grid pictures (flat grid, grid in
perspective). Orientation is the red/white diamond compass. The track number is white on a dark grey box ("3E").
The Turn Automation panel is a translucent dark box with a turn arrow, the distance ("10 ft") and the passes.

**Alerts** (p. 43): a black box with a round "!" icon, bold title in capitals ("AUTOTRAC DISENGAGED"), the
message underneath, and a yellow ✕ button.

**Title bar** (p. 5): the run page name on the left, then a dark green rounded **status pill** (clock, weather,
GNSS, correction badge such as "RTK", signal bars), then the page arrows.

**Menu** (p. 6): left tabs Machine Settings / Applications / System (selected tab yellow with an arrow). Apps are
white rounded tiles with a coloured picture and the yellow/green stripe; every app has its own picture.

**Layout Manager** (pp. 10, 15, 16): left tabs Run Page Sets / Shortcut Bars / All Run Pages (selected yellow).
List rows are cream gradients with an icon, a bold name and a count line; the selected row turns yellow and shows
small **yellow square buttons**: edit (pencil), duplicate, delete (trash). The bottom strip has "+ Add New" (yellow
square plus). Module editing uses a yellow round Move control and a cream Remove button with a trash icon.

**Side modules** (p. 5): Location, Season averages, and **Next Track** with a Swap button.

## The overhaul

1. Shortcut keys: strips only on toggle keys; Deere-style LED + AUTO across the top; blue pie; new QUICK LINE and
   AUTO TURN keys; redrawn icons for every key (original line drawings).
2. App icons: a distinct picture for every Menu app, with a colour accent like the G5's tiles.
3. Map: round olive buttons with a light ring; overhead/perspective grid icons instead of "2D/3D"; track number
   box white on dark grey.
4. Alerts: G5 black shade with the "!" disc, a bold title line and a yellow ✕.
5. Title bar: dark green status pill (clock, weather, GNSS, correction badge, bars).
6. Layout Manager: Deere list rows (yellow selected row with yellow square edit / duplicate / delete), "+ Add New",
   yellow Add, round Move and the trash Remove button.
7. Later: a Next Track side module with Swap, split map, flags and the Turn Automation map panel.
